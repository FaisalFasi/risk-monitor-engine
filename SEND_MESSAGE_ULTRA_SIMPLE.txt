Q: How do I send a message to a smart contract?

A: You just call a function! Like this:

    await contract.deposit({ token: 'NEAR', amount: '10' });
           ↑
           THIS IS THE MESSAGE


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  THINK OF IT LIKE TEXTING A FRIEND
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Regular Text Message:
  1. Open phone
  2. Type: "Hey, can you save $10 for me?"
  3. Press SEND
  4. Friend receives message
  5. Friend: "Done! You now have $10 saved"

Smart Contract "Message":
  1. Connect wallet
  2. Call function: contract.deposit({ amount: '10' })
  3. Approve in wallet popup
  4. Blockchain receives "message"
  5. Smart contract: "Done! You now have 10 shares"

IT'S THE SAME THING!
